Takachiho Gorge rental boat — 19 Nov opens 5 Nov · shuts 17 Nov 09:00

The window opens exactly two weeks ahead and closes 9:00 AM two days before your slot. Online only — no phone, no time-slot requests, no waitlist. Same-day tickets are sold at the gorge reception in the morning only. Operating hours are decided each morning around 8:00 on water level and weather.

Nabegataki Falls — 14:00 or 15:00 on the 17th refundable until 14 Nov

Advance online booking only, in timed slots. ¥300 an adult, 9:00–17:00 with last entry 16:30, capped at 40 minutes to an hour with no re-entry. Book it for arrival day rather than the 18th — that swap is what makes the crater fit.

20Fri

Car back at ten, wheels up at five

Car 10:00 · flight 17:00

  • Leave Miyazaki by 07:45The drive is 1 h 50 m and the car is due at 10:00. That is the whole morning gone, so no Aoshima on the way out — which is exactly why the coast belongs on the 19th.
  • Return the car in the city, not at the airportThe airport sits 36 minutes out in Kirishima. Hand the car back there at 10:00 and you spend seven hours in a departure hall. Hand it back in Kagoshima and you get a free day instead.
  • Sakurajima — 10:30 to 15:00A live volcano straight across the bay, ferry about 15 minutes and running constantly. Six free hours is the right amount of time for it, and it costs no extra night.
  • Airport by 15:5051 minutes by limousine bus from Kagoshima-Chuo, 36 by car. Leaving the city at 15:00 lands you there around 15:50 for a 17:00 flight.

If you add more days

Everything on the map's could add layer, grouped by where the extra night actually goes. Nothing here needs the route redrawn.

A night at the front, before KurokawaThe Oita onsen belt

  • Yufuin — smart, walkable onsen town with a lake and a long craft street. Sits almost exactly on the Fukuoka–Kurokawa line, so it costs you nothing in distance.
  • Beppu — the big one: steam vents across a whole hillside and eight distinct hot-spring fields. Roughly an hour east of Kurokawa.

A night in the middle, around AsoCaldera and castle

  • Takamori & Minamiaso — the caldera's southern floor, and the moss-covered cave shrine at Kamishikimi Kumano Imasu. This is also where the spare beds are, as the map shows.
  • Kumamoto Castle — one of Japan's three great castles, about an hour west of Aso. A detour rather than a stop, so it wants its own half-day.
  • Gokase — not a sight, but 14 km from Takachiho and the closest place with rooms if the town stays full.

A night at the end, before KagoshimaKirishima and the bay

  • Kirishima Jingu — a shrine in deep cedar forest on the volcano's flank, close to Kagoshima airport. The single best way to spend an extra night here, because it also de-risks a morning departure.
  • Ebino Highlands — crater lakes on the rim above Kirishima, and genuine autumn colour in November.
  • Sakurajima — a live volcano directly across the bay from Kagoshima, reached by a ferry that runs through the night.
  • Sekinoo Falls — basalt columns and giant potholes, sitting right on the Miyazaki–Kagoshima line, so nearly free.

Or stretch the coast dayFurther down Nichinan

  • Cape Toi — wild horses grazing on an open headland at the southern end of the Nichinan coast, below Obi.
  • Nobeoka — the coastal gateway, if you would rather reach the sea on the 18th than the 19th.

Where does the car go back — city or airport?

The last thing that changes the shape of a day. City means Sakurajima fills the 20th. Airport means seven hours in a departure hall, and that morning would be better spent driving to Kirishima instead.

2026 Kumamoto Earthquake advisory

The Takachiho tourism board carries an advisory dated 14 August. It reports no damage in Takachiho itself — it asks anyone travelling to Kumamoto Prefecture to confirm that accommodation and attractions are operating, via the Japan Tourism Agency and JNTO status pages. Kurokawa and Aso are inside that prefecture. Every leg here routed normally, which suggests the main roads are open, but that is not confirmation. Verify before anything becomes non-refundable.
